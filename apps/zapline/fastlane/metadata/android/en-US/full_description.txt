Zapline plays the sources you add: Xtream-compatible logins, M3U playlists and XMLTV TV guides. It is built for the TV remote first and works on Android TV, Fire TV (Fire OS), phones and tablets.

Everything is free:
* unlimited sources and guides
* TV guide grid with catch-up where your provider offers it
* favourites, search, number-key zapping
* add a source from your phone by scanning a QR code
* backup and restore through your home network

Zapline does not provide any content. It ships with no playlists, channels or links, and does not endorse any provider. You are responsible for the sources you add.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Source code under the MIT licence.
